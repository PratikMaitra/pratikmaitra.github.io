I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Iowa State University, advised by Professor Qi Li. My research focuses on weakly supervised methods for natural language processing and data mining, with an emphasis on event extraction, event annotation, and event ontology creation.

I also collaborate with Professor Sena Chae at the University of Iowa to identify symptoms in clinical notes from the electronic health records of patients with acute myeloid leukemia (AML). I previously collaborated with Professors Tong Wang and Bijaya Adhikari at the University of Iowa.

I plan to pursue an academic career and am interested in research opportunities and assistant professor positions.
