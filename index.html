Research Interests

My research focuses on information extraction and retrieval using natural language processing, particularly weak and distant supervision for information retrieval.

At the University of Iowa, I collaborated with Professors Tong Wang, Sena Chae, and Bijaya Adhikari on machine learning projects, including k-nearest-neighbor methods for graphs derived from hospital CAD drawings and named entity recognition in clinical notes.
